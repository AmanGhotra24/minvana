# Minvana Brand Kit

Master brand assets. SVGs are the source of truth — export PNG/JPG from these
when a platform needs raster (any free converter works; keep exports next to the SVGs).

## Assets
- `minvana-wordmark.svg` — lowercase bold wordmark (ink black). Website header, video end-cards, docs.
- `minvana-60-icon.svg` — electric-blue rounded square with white "60". App icon, social profile pictures, favicon.
- `minvana-lockup.svg` — icon + wordmark side by side. About page, press, partnerships.

## Identity
- **Wordmark:** lowercase `minvana`, bold geometric sans (Inter / SF Pro / system-ui), tight tracking.
- **Mark:** the "60" — the one-minute promise as a symbol. Works alone as an app icon.
- **Voice:** sharp, modern, confident. A product, not a publication.
- **Colors:**
  - Background: white `#FFFFFF`
  - Ink: `#111111`
  - Accent (one only): electric blue `#2563EB`
  - Dividers/muted: soft gray `#E5E7EB` / `#6B7280`
- **Headlines:** bold modern sans, tight tracking. Body: clean readable sans.

## Signature format (the moat vs InShorts)
Every story: "Your 60 Seconds" 3-bullet box → timestamped sections
(0:00 The Hook, 0:20 The Context, 0:40 The Twist, 1:00 The Takeaway) →
read, watch, or listen.
