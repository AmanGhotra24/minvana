About minvana

minute + nirvana = Minvana

"Enlightenment in a minute."

minvana is a simple idea: the world is complicated, but understanding it shouldn't take all day. We break down money, tech, science, history, and human behavior into short, plain-language stories — no jargon, no hype, no ten-minute lectures.

Our home is YouTube Shorts, where every story lands in under a minute. This site is the companion shelf: the same big ideas, given a little more room to breathe, in simple English. Every story comes in three forms — read, watch, or listen — engineered into 60 seconds.

How we work

Each story starts with real reporting from established news outlets — you'll find them credited in the Sources section of every article. We write our own original scripts and articles; facts come from journalists, words come from us. We present competing claims side by side and let you decide what to think.

AI-content disclosure

In the interest of full transparency: the voiceovers and visuals in Minvana's YouTube videos are AI-generated. The research, scripting, and writing are human-directed, and every factual claim is traceable to a credited source. If we ever get something wrong, we'll correct it.

For education and entertainment

minvana is made for education and entertainment only. Nothing here — especially anything touching markets, money, or investing — is financial advice. Do your own research, and talk to a qualified professional before making decisions with your money.
