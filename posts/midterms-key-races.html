Key Races to Watch & How Control Shifts

October 2026 — United States

Your 60 seconds

Control of the Senate and the House comes down to a narrow group of competitive battleground states and toss-up districts. The Senate races to watch: Georgia, Michigan, New Hampshire, Maine, North Carolina, Ohio, Iowa, and Alaska.

0:00 — The hook

All 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats are on the ballot in the 2026 midterms, but control of the entire U.S. government hinges on just a fraction of them. Here is the 2026 battlefield, in sixty seconds.

0:20 — The context

In the Senate, Republicans are defending 22 seats while Democrats defend 13. According to analysis from Ballotpedia and the Cook Political Report, the path to the majority starts with key battlegrounds Democrats are fighting to hold — specifically Georgia, plus open seats in Michigan and New Hampshire.

0:40 — The twist

To actually flip the Senate, challengers have to go on offense. Coverage from CBS News 2026 Midterm Election Races highlights intense contests in Maine, North Carolina, Ohio, Iowa, and Alaska. Meanwhile, in the House, 74 members are heading for the exits — the most pre-election departures in over three decades — leaving 21 pure toss-up seats that will decide the chamber by a razor-thin margin. In our final part, we'll cover how to track the forecasts and official results.

1:00 — The takeaway

  • Control of the Senate and House comes down to a narrow group of competitive battleground states and toss-up districts.
  • Key Senate contests to watch include Georgia, Michigan, New Hampshire, Maine, North Carolina, Ohio, Iowa, and Alaska.
  • Follow race ratings at Ballotpedia and find official state election portals at USAGov.
